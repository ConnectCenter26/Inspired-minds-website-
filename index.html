Common questions

What homeowners ask before they call.

Are you licensed and insured?+
We carry general liability insurance, and we're glad to share full license and insurance details directly once we've discussed your project. We handle that one-on-one rather than posting it publicly.
How is this different from just hiring a contractor directly?+
The inspection and estimate come first, independently, before any contractor is chosen — so you're working from a real baseline instead of a single contractor's opening bid. We're not paid by the contractor, so there's no incentive to inflate scope.
What if I'm not happy with the finished work?+
Every engagement ends with an independent audit and quality assurance sign-off before the project — and final payment — closes out. Work gets checked against the original scope and spec, not just eyeballed.
How much does this cost?+
Every project is scoped individually based on the property and the work involved. You'll get a clear, itemized estimate before anything begins — no obligation to move forward after the initial inspection.
Do I pay Inspired Minds and the contractor separately?+
It depends on the service. For consulting work — inspections, estimates, project management, or audits — you pay Inspired Minds directly. For general contracting projects Inspired Minds takes on and delivers end to end, it's a single contract.
How fast do you respond to inquiries?+
Reach out by phone, email, or the form on this site — we follow up to schedule a consultation as quickly as the project allows.
