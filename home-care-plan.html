The Handy Home Care Plan

Seasonal maintenance visits for South Denver metro homes, built around what Colorado actually does to a house. Month-to-month. Cancel with 30 days' notice.

Front Range homes take a beating every year: freeze-thaw swings, spring hail, dry winters and expansive clay soil that moves with every wet season. Most expensive repairs start as small things nobody looked at. A clogged downspout dumping water at the foundation. A hose bib that froze. Caulk the altitude sun cooked off.

The Care Plan puts your home on a schedule. We come out each season, work through a Colorado-specific checklist, handle small fixes during the visit, and leave you a written report with photos. Anything bigger gets a quote at member pricing, and you decide.

What we check

A checklist written for the Front Range.

Essentials covers spring and fall. Plus adds summer and winter.

Spring

After freeze-thaw and hail season
  • Roof edges, gutters and flashing checked for hail damage (with photos)
  • Gutters and downspouts cleared, extensions carrying water away from the foundation
  • Grading and new foundation cracks noted (expansive soil)
  • Outdoor spigots tested for freeze damage
  • Exterior caulk, paint, seals and screens
  • Smoke and CO detectors tested, filter replaced

Fall

Winterize before the first hard freeze
  • Outdoor spigots shut off, drained and covered
  • Sprinkler blowout confirmed
  • Gutters cleared after leaf drop
  • Gaps at exterior penetrations sealed
  • Weatherstripping, door sweeps, sump pump (if present)
  • Furnace filter replaced, detectors tested, crawlspace checked for moisture

Summer · Plus

Heat, UV and outdoor living
  • AC condensate line and outdoor unit area
  • Deck and railing tightness and wood condition
  • Tub, shower and sink caulk
  • Under-sink leak check, dryer vent lint check
  • Attic ventilation look-over

Winter · Plus

Mid-season check-in
  • Attic check for ice-dam signs and frost
  • Sticking doors and drywall cracks from seasonal soil movement
  • GFCI outlets tested
  • Water heater area checked for leaks
  • Small-repair list built for spring

How it works

  1. Request to join through the contact form below, or call 303-204-2710. Tell us your address and which plan you want.
  2. We confirm your plan and set up simple monthly billing. There's no payment on this website.
  3. We schedule your first visit, then keep you on the seasonal calendar.

Plan terms

  • Month-to-month, with no long-term contract. Cancel anytime with 30 days' notice.
  • Visits are scheduled within each season's window. Unused visits don't roll over.
  • Parts and materials beyond small consumables are quoted and billed separately. Work beyond the visit checklist is quoted at the member rate.
  • Not included: work that requires a licensed trade (electrical, plumbing beyond basic fixture-level fixes, gas, HVAC refrigerant, backflow testing), roofing repairs, major repairs, and anything needing a permit. We quote these separately or refer you to a qualified pro.
  • The visit report is a maintenance checklist, not a home inspection, engineering opinion or guarantee.
  • Service area: Parker, Highlands Ranch, Littleton, Lone Tree and nearby.
